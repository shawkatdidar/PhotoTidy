PHOTO TIDY - first open

1. Drag "Photo Tidy" into the Applications folder and open it.

2. Use "Check this Mac" and "Check access" on the welcome screen. If library
   access is blocked, enable Photo Tidy in System Settings > Privacy & Security
   > Full Disk Access, then reopen the app.

3. Download Model gets EmbeddingGemma 2 (about 485 MB, once). You can scan
   and search offline after that. Photos album access is requested only when
   you create a review album. Your photos stay on this Mac.

Photo Tidy never deletes or edits photos. It creates a "Tidy Review" album;
you review it in Photos and decide what to delete there.
