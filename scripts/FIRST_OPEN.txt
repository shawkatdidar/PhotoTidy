PHOTO TIDY - how to open it the first time

1. Drag "Photo Tidy" into the Applications folder.

2. This build is not signed or notarized by Apple, so macOS may block the first
   launch. Try opening it once, then go to System Settings > Privacy & Security,
   scroll down, and click "Open Anyway". Only open software you trust.

3. The welcome screen shows a three-step guide and an access check. If library access is
   blocked, enable Photo Tidy in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Full Disk Access.
   Download Model in the app gets EmbeddingGemma 2 (485 MB, once). You can scan offline
   after that. Explore searches photos by phrase or visual match; Collections groups
   scenes and events locally. Photos album access is only requested when you create a review album.
   Your photos never leave this Mac.

Photo Tidy never deletes or edits photos. It creates a "Tidy Review" album; you review it in
Photos and delete there (Recently Deleted keeps items for 30 days).
